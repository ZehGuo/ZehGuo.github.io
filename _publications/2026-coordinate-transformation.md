This paper introduces a coordinate-transformation method for simplifying stability analysis of Nash equilibrium sets in piecewise dynamical systems.

The paper was presented in the **Game-Theoretic Methods in Control II** session as paper **FrA4.2** at ECC 2026 in Reykjavík, Iceland. See the [official ECC 2026 program](https://controls.papercept.net/conferences/conferences/ECC26/program/ECC26_ContentListWeb_3.html) and the [IEEE Xplore record](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11625521).
