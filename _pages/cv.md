## Research experience

### Postdoctoral Researcher, Institute of Science Tokyo, May 2025–March 2026

- Explored fair allocation of benefits and costs in shared photovoltaic-battery systems.
- Investigated cooperative-game-theoretic and axiomatic formulations, including relationships between equal allocation and the Shapley value.
- Considered opinion-dynamics models as a way to describe how fairness preferences may evolve among participants.

### Doctoral Researcher, Tokyo Institute of Technology / Institute of Science Tokyo, October 2021–March 2025

- Developed a theoretical framework for noncooperative dynamical systems with vector-valued payoffs.
- Established results on the characterization, topology, and stability of Nash equilibrium sets.
- Designed incentive mechanisms for Pareto improvement in multi-objective dynamical systems.
- Published this research in IEEE Control Systems Letters and major international control conferences, including IFAC World Congress, IEEE CDC, ACC, and ECC.

### Graduate Researcher, Zhejiang University, 2017–2020

- Studied multi-agent network systems with dynamic edges.
- Developed a cone-based synthesis method related to passivity and dissipativity.
- Analyzed gain and phase margins, consensus, and cooperative output regulation; published the results at the Chinese Automation Congress.

### Selected Undergraduate Projects, Zhejiang University, 2014–2017

- **Bachelor's thesis - Distribution of PMU for Power System State Estimation:** studied phasor measurement unit placement for power-system state estimation.
- **Student Research Training Program - Home Security System Design Based on Power Line:** developed a prototype using ATmega128 and ATmega8 microcontrollers for sensor input, alarm transmission, message display, and timing functions.
