Research experience
======

### Postdoctoral Researcher, Institute of Science Tokyo, 2025–2026

- Studied fair energy management for shared photovoltaic battery systems.
- Applied cooperative game theory and axiomatic allocation methods to resource-allocation problems.
- Investigated relationships between equal allocation, the Shapley value, and broader families of fair solutions.

### Doctoral Researcher, Tokyo Institute of Technology / Institute of Science Tokyo, 2021–2025

- Characterized Nash equilibrium sets in noncooperative games with vector-valued payoffs.
- Analyzed compactness, connectedness, and stability properties of equilibrium sets.
- Developed methods for Pareto improvement and incentive design in multi-objective dynamical systems.
- Proposed a coordinate-transformation method for stability analysis of piecewise dynamical systems.

### Graduate Researcher, Zhejiang University, 2017–2020

- Studied multi-agent network systems with dynamic edges.
- Developed a cone-based control synthesis method and analyzed gain and phase margins.
- Worked on consensus and cooperative output regulation.
